Support

How do I restore purchases? Open Shop > Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple or Google store account. Wait for network verification. Cross-store transfer is unavailable.

What does Remove Ads do? It removes forced ads. Eligible rewarded actions become direct claims with the same limits and cooldowns.

Why is an ad unavailable? Consent, age gate, network, fill, cap or cooldown may prevent it; no reward is promised for an unavailable ad.

How do I request a refund? Use the purchase history and refund flow of the Apple or Google store that charged you. Do not send a full receipt or payment card in a support message.

What if progress or purchases look wrong? Restart, check network and store account, then contact ivyh0330@gmail.com with platform, app version, device/OS, time and platform order reference.

Privacy/data requests: ivyh0330@gmail.com. Public policy: Privacy Policy.
