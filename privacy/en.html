Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06. Controller/publisher: yi-wei Hsieh. Contact: ivyh0330@gmail.com.

The game stores progress, settings and purchase entitlements in app-private local storage. Purchases use Unity IAP and Apple or Google stores. Signed transaction evidence or purchase tokens, product and app identity are sent securely to our CommerceVerifier service to grant, restore and audit purchases and refunds. The server keeps a transaction ledger and refund-event journal. We retain transaction records only as long as reasonably necessary for transaction verification, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting and legal obligations.

When Google Mobile Ads is initialized and UMP consent and regional settings allow ad requests, Google Mobile Ads and Google UMP may process device identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, ad and product interactions, consent state, and diagnostic information such as crash, performance and other diagnostics. These data may be used for third-party advertising, developer advertising, analytics, and app functionality as described by the SDKs. Some categories are linked to a device or user; the Google Mobile Ads iOS privacy manifest declares its Device ID data for tracking. The app requests Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission before initializing Google Mobile Ads on iOS. If permission is denied or restricted, ad requests use Google's non-personalized publisher privacy treatment and the Google publisher first-party ID is disabled; if permission is not determined, ads do not initialize. UMP consent and applicable regional settings still govern whether ad requests can be made. Apple and Google process payments under their own policies. Players cannot create accounts; the game does not request contacts, health data or precise location.

An optional Google Play review-only feature lets authorized store reviewers use the four A1 permanent benefits for up to 30 minutes. Review credentials are sent over HTTPS and processed in memory only to verify access. The app and verifier do not store the credentials, create a player account, or write a purchase transaction. Review entitlements remain in device memory, expire automatically, and can be ended in the app; the publisher can revoke future access by rotating or disabling the reviewer credentials. This feature is not a general player sign-in or purchase bypass.

You may use platform settings and in-app privacy choices when offered to change ad consent. Eligible purchases can be restored through the same store account. You may clear local progress by removing app data, subject to platform behavior. For server data access/deletion requests, contact ivyh0330@gmail.com. Transaction records may need to be retained for the purposes described above.

Data categories and third parties: local game save (local only); purchase history and transaction evidence and refund ledger (our verifier, Apple/Google); advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad/product interactions, diagnostics, and consent data (Google Mobile Ads/UMP, according to device, region, ATT status and consent choices).
